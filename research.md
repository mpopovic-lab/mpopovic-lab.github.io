## Research Interests

My work combines theoretical physics with biological problems, with particular focus on:

- **Tissue Mechanics and Morphogenesis**: Understanding how tissues fold, flow, and shape themselves during development
- **Plasticity and Yielding**: Investigating mechanical properties of soft and amorphous materials, from epithelial tissues to glass-forming systems
- **Multi-scale Modeling**: Connecting cellular-level processes to tissue-scale dynamics
